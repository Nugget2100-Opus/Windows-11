# Windows-11
Windows 11 but accessible in your browser! Not everything is proper working or complete. Updates MAY happen.

Currently there are:
- 14 apps (Terminal, To Do, VS Code, Discord, Whiteboard and Word, PowerPoint, OneNote, Mail, Store, Your Phone, Spotify, Twitter and GitHub)
- System: Display, Sound, Notifications, Focus Assist, Power & battery, Storage, Recovery and About.
- Other: Bluetooth & Devices, Network & Internet, Personalization (Lock Screen, Themes, Fonts, Start, Taskbar), Apps, Accounts, Time & Language, Gaming, Accessibility, Privacy & Security and Windows Update.
- Other fun and working features!
